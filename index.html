Complete Lifecycle Test Walkthrough

Follow these steps in multiple tabs to verify full cross-window synchronization:

1
Admin Provisions Table

In Admin → Tables, click "Add Table" (e.g. Table 11). System creates table and automatically generates QR.

2
Customer Scans & Orders

Customer opens customer.html?table=11. Adds Chicken Biryani × 2, notes "Less spicy", and places order.

3
Admin Accepts & Kitchen Prepares

Admin clicks "Accept". Kitchen KDS receives ticket, clicks "Start Preparing", then "Mark Ready".

4
Waiter Serves & Payment

Waiter clicks "Serve Order". Customer UI unlocks Bill. Customer selects "Pay Online", settles bill & prints Tax Invoice.
